Casual Chinese Conversations

How people actually talk. Not textbook Chinese.

How to use this:
  • Read one story a day, out loud, twice.
  • First pass with pinyin. Second pass tap Pinyin to turn it off and see what sticks.
  • Have your mom read the other person's lines so you're actually responding to someone.
  • The box at the bottom of each story is the stuff that won't be in a textbook. Memorize those whole, don't break them down.
  • Print lays it out one story per page. Tap a part below to jump to it.
